1. Overview

ProMix DJ Studio is a fully self-contained DJ mixing application delivered as a single HTML file. Open it in any modern web browser — no software installation, no app store, no subscription. Everything runs locally on your machine using the Web Audio API.

It was designed by analyzing and improving upon the world's best DJ software: DJ.Studio, Algoriddim djay Pro, VirtualDJ, and Fadr — combining the best features of all four into one free, portable tool.

3. Quick Start Guide

Get mixing in under 2 minutes:

1
Open the file

Download dj-mixer.html from GitHub. Double-click it — it opens in your default browser. No installation needed.

2
Load Track A

Click the 📂 Load button on Deck A (left side). Select any MP3 or audio file from your computer. The waveform will appear and BPM/key will be detected automatically.

3
Load Track B

Click the 📂 Load button on Deck B (right side). Load a second track.

4
Play Deck A

Press the large green ▶ Play button on Deck A, or press Space on your keyboard. The vinyl will spin and audio plays.

5
Sync and mix

Press SYNC on Deck B to match its tempo to Deck A. Press Play on Deck B. Slide the crossfader from left to right to blend between tracks.

6
Record your mix

Click ● REC in the top bar before you start mixing. Click ⏹ STOP REC when done. Click ⬇ Download Mix to save the file locally.

✅
You're mixing! That's all it takes for a basic mix. Read on to explore all the professional features.

5. Loading Tracks

Method 1: Local File Upload

1
Click 📂 Load on your chosen deck

The file browser opens. Navigate to your music folder.

2
Select your audio file

Supported formats: MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, AAC, M4A, and any format your browser supports.

3
Wait for analysis

The app decodes the audio, draws the waveform, and automatically detects BPM and musical key. This takes 1–3 seconds depending on file size.

4
Check the readout

Verify the BPM and KEY values appear in the info row. If BPM looks wrong, use TAP tempo to correct it.

Method 2: YouTube (with API Key)

See the YouTube Search section for full instructions.

⚠️
Large files Files over 100 MB may take several seconds to decode. Very large WAV files (24-bit, 192 kHz) may cause a brief pause. MP3s at 320kbps load fastest.

7. BPM & Key Detection

Automatic BPM Detection

When you load a track, ProMix analyzes the audio waveform using onset energy detection — it finds sudden increases in loudness (beats) and calculates the average interval between them to determine the tempo.

The detected BPM appears next to the deck label. BPM is always clamped to a musical range (70–200 BPM), so double-time and half-time tracks are handled correctly.

Correcting BPM with Tap Tempo

If the auto-detected BPM is wrong (common with complex electronic music or live recordings):

1
Play the track

Listen to the beat.

2
Click TAP repeatedly in time with the beat

Click at least 4 times. The BPM updates after each tap using the average interval.

3
Tap again after 2+ seconds to reset

A 2-second gap clears the previous taps and starts fresh.

Automatic Key Detection

ProMix uses a pitch class profile analysis (based on the Krumhansl–Schmuckler model) to detect the musical key. It samples the frequency spectrum, builds a 12-note chroma profile, and compares it to known major and minor key templates.

The detected key is displayed in Camelot Wheel notation (e.g., 8A = A minor, 8B = C major).

Key Shift

The – and + buttons shift the key display (and playback pitch) by semitones. This is useful when:

  • You want to force two tracks into the same key
  • The auto-detection was slightly off
  • You want to creatively pitch a track up or down
ℹ️
Camelot Notation: Numbers 1–12 represent key families. "A" = minor keys, "B" = major keys. Adjacent numbers on the wheel (e.g., 7A and 8A) are harmonically compatible.

8. Camelot Wheel

Click the ⟳ Camelot Wheel button in the header to open the interactive harmonic mixing guide. This SVG wheel shows all 24 musical keys arranged so that harmonically compatible keys are adjacent.

Reading the Wheel

RingMeaning
Outer ring (B keys)Major keys (e.g., 8B = C major, 9B = G major)
Inner ring (A keys)Minor keys (e.g., 8A = A minor, 9A = E minor)

9. EQ & Mixing

3-Band Equalizer

Each deck has three frequency bands controlled by the canvas knobs in the mixer center:

BandFrequency RangeControls
HI (High shelf)10,000 Hz and aboveCymbals, hi-hats, air, sibilance
MID (Peaking)Around 1,000 HzVocals, guitars, presence
LO (Low shelf)200 Hz and belowKick drum, bass, sub frequencies

How to use the knobs

  • Drag up on a knob to boost that frequency range (+12 dB max)
  • Drag down to cut that frequency range (–12 dB max)
  • Scroll the mouse wheel on a knob to adjust it precisely
  • Knob at center position = flat (no boost or cut)
💡
Classic EQ Transition Technique: Before bringing in Deck B, cut the lows (LO knob all the way down) on Deck B. As you move the crossfader to B, gradually bring the lows back up. This prevents bass clash between the two tracks.

10. Crossfader

The horizontal crossfader blends between Deck A and Deck B. When the fader is:

  • Far left — Only Deck A is heard
  • Center — Both decks play at equal volume
  • Far right — Only Deck B is heard

Crossfader Curves

ModeBest ForBehavior
LinearGeneral mixingSmooth, proportional blend across the full range
ScratchHip-hop, turntablismQuick cut — audio switches sharply near the edges, flat in the middle
SmoothDeep house, ambientCosine curve — gradual fade that sounds natural to the ear

12. YouTube Search

Getting a Free YouTube API Key

The YouTube search feature requires a free API key from Google. This takes about 5 minutes:

1
Go to Google Cloud Console

Visit console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Google account.

2
Create a new project

Click the project dropdown at the top → New Project → name it "DJ Mixer" → click Create.

3
Enable YouTube Data API v3

Go to APIs & Services → Library → search "YouTube Data API v3" → click Enable.

4
Create credentials

Go to APIs & Services → Credentials → click Create Credentials → API Key. Copy the key that appears.

5
Paste it in ProMix

In the ProMix header bar, paste your key into the API Key field. Your searches are now live.

ℹ️
Free quota: Google gives you 10,000 search units per day for free — roughly 100 searches. More than enough for personal DJ use. No credit card required.

Searching for a Track

1
Type your search in the search bar

Examples: "Daft Punk Around The World", "Drake Hotline Bling remix", "deep house 128 bpm"

2
Press Enter or click SEARCH

Results appear with thumbnails, title, and channel name.

3
Choose an action for each result:
  • → A — Load directly to Deck A (via cobalt.tools audio extraction)
  • → B — Load directly to Deck B
  • ⬇ MP3 — Download as MP3 file to your computer
  • ▶ YT — Open the video on YouTube in a new tab

Downloading MP3s

The MP3 download uses cobalt.tools — a free, open-source YouTube-to-audio service. No account or login is needed.

1
Find a track via YouTube Search

Or have a YouTube URL ready.

2
Click ⬇ MP3

The app requests a download link from cobalt.tools.

3
The MP3 file downloads

Your browser may ask where to save it, or it saves to your Downloads folder automatically.

4
Load the downloaded file into ProMix

Use the 📂 Load button to load your newly downloaded MP3 to a deck.

⚠️
Important: Only download music you have the right to use. Respect copyright law. ProMix DJ Studio is intended for personal use, private events, and licensed DJ performances.

13. Recording Your Mix

ProMix can record everything coming out of the master output — all your mixing, effects, and transitions — and save it as an audio file you can share or archive.

1
Before you start mixing, click ● REC

The button turns red and pulses. Recording has started. Everything you do from this point will be captured.

2
Perform your mix

Load tracks, play, crossfade, apply effects — mix as normal. The recorder captures it all.

3
Click ⏹ STOP REC when finished

The button returns to normal. Your mix is now stored in memory.

4
Click ⬇ Download Mix

A file named ProMix_[timestamp].webm downloads to your computer. This is a standard audio file playable in VLC, Chrome, and most media players.

Converting to MP3

The downloaded file is in WebM/Opus format — a high-quality audio format. To convert to MP3:

  • VLC Media Player — Media → Convert/Save → Add file → choose MP3 output
  • Audacity — File → Import → export as MP3
  • Online converter — cloudconvert.com (free)
  • FFmpeg (command line): ffmpeg -i ProMix_123.webm output.mp3

14. Auto Mix

The ⚡ Auto Mix feature automates the transition between Deck A and Deck B — it syncs the BPM and smoothly moves the crossfader over about 4 seconds.

1
Load tracks on both Deck A and Deck B

Make sure both tracks are loaded and BPM is detected.

2
Start playing Deck A

Let the track play to the point where you want to mix out.

3
Click ⚡ Auto Mix

The app automatically: (a) syncs Deck B's tempo to Deck A, (b) starts Deck B playing, (c) smoothly fades the crossfader from A to B over ~4 seconds.

💡
Pro Tip: For a better auto-mix, use the Camelot Wheel to choose a harmonically compatible track for Deck B before triggering Auto Mix. Then position Deck B to its intro or drop before clicking Auto Mix.

15. Stem Separation

Stems are the individual components of a song: Vocals, Drums, Bass, and Melody. By separating them, you can create acapellas, instrumentals, or completely remix a track.

Stem Volume Controls

The Stems panel at the bottom of the app lets you adjust the volume of each component independently. The toggle button mutes/unmutes that stem. The slider adjusts its level.

AI Stem Separation via Fadr

1
Click ⚗ Separate Stems (AI)

This opens Fadr.com in a new tab.

2
Upload your track to Fadr

Drag and drop your audio file onto the Fadr interface.

3
Wait for AI processing (under 60 seconds)

Fadr splits the track into individual stems: Vocals, Drums, Bass, Melody (and up to 16 components on Pro).

4
Download the individual stems

Download the acapella (vocals only), instrumental, or any individual stem.

5
Load stems back into ProMix

Load the acapella from one song onto Deck A and the instrumental from another onto Deck B — you've just made a mashup!

16. Sync & Pitch

SYNC Button

Click SYNC on a deck to automatically match its BPM to the other deck. This adjusts the pitch slider percentage to make both decks play at the same tempo.

Pitch Slider

The horizontal pitch slider adjusts playback speed from –8% to +8%. Drag right to speed up, left to slow down. The percentage is shown to the right of the slider.

Key Shift Buttons

The – and + buttons shift the pitch by one semitone per click. This changes both the key display and the actual playback pitch. Useful for matching keys before mixing.

19. Tutorial 2 — DJ Party Set with Recording

Goal: Record a 3-track mix with effects. Time: ~15 minutes.

1
Prepare your tracklist

Choose 3 songs with similar BPM (e.g., all around 120–130 BPM). Check their keys using the Camelot Wheel to pick harmonically compatible tracks.

Load Track 1 on Deck A and play it

8

9
Click ⏹ STOP REC and then ⬇ Download Mix

22. FAQ

No sound is coming out. What's wrong?

Browsers require a user interaction before playing audio. Click the Play button or anywhere on the page first. Also check your system volume and that the browser tab isn't muted.

The BPM detected is half or double the actual tempo

This is common with some genres. Use the TAP button to correct it manually — tap along to the beat 4–8 times.

YouTube search returns "Error"

Your API key may be invalid, expired, or over the daily quota. Check the key in Google Cloud Console. The free daily quota is 10,000 units (~100 searches).

MP3 download doesn't start

cobalt.tools may be temporarily busy. Try again in a moment. Alternatively, click ▶ YT to open the video on YouTube and use another download tool.

Can I use this app offline?

Yes — all core mixing features work completely offline. You only need internet for YouTube search and MP3 download.

Can I save my mix settings or hot cues?

Currently, settings are not saved between sessions. Hot cues, BPM edits, and loaded tracks reset when you refresh the page. Save your recording before closing.

The file doesn't open on my computer

Right-click the dj-mixer.html file → Open with → choose Google Chrome, Firefox, or Microsoft Edge.

Can I use this on my phone or tablet?

Yes — the app runs in mobile browsers. However, the full layout is designed for a laptop/desktop screen. Landscape mode on a tablet is recommended.
